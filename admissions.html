Eligibility

Nursery Teacher Training (NTT)

Class 10th + Any Degree passed in any stream. Minimum age 16th + to any age limits. Candidates awaiting results may apply provisionally.

Teacher Training Workshop

Open to working teachers, teaching aspirants and school staff. No formal qualification required.

Individual & Marital Counselling

Open to all - no eligibility criteria. By appointment.

Career Guidance

Open to students from Class 10 upwards, parents, and working adults considering a change.

No Entrance Exam Required

No entrance examination is required for any of our programmes. Candidates who have completed schooling through open or distance boards are eligible for the NTT course.
