01 NURSERY TEACHER TRAINING

Nursery Teacher Training (NTT)

Our flagship programme prepares you to teach children in the 2–6 age group with confidence. The NTT course covers early childhood development, pre-primary teaching methodology, classroom management, phonics and early literacy, number readiness, art and craft, rhymes and storytelling, and child psychology basics.

You will not only learn what to teach but how young children actually learn - why a four-year-old needs movement built into a lesson, how to handle a crying child on day one, and how to plan a week that keeps twenty toddlers engaged. The course includes practical teaching practice and observation so you graduate with real classroom hours behind you, not just notes.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

  • Early childhood care and development foundations
  • Pre-primary teaching methods and lesson planning
  • Phonics, early literacy and pre-writing skills
  • Number readiness and pre-maths concepts
  • Classroom management for the 2–6 age group
  • Art, craft, rhymes, storytelling and activity design
  • Child psychology and behaviour handling
  • Parent communication and school documentation

02 TEACHER TRAINING WORKSHOP

Teacher Training Workshop

Short, intensive workshops for teachers already in the classroom - and for schools that want their whole team working from the same playbook. Where the NTT course builds a career from scratch, these workshops sharpen specific skills fast.

Topics are built around what teachers actually struggle with: managing a disruptive class without raising your voice, making a dry topic engaging, designing activities with almost no budget, assessing young children fairly, and handling difficult parent conversations. Sessions are hands-on, with role-play, live demonstration and peer feedback rather than slide-heavy lectures.

FORMATS AVAILABLE

Individual enrolment
Join a scheduled open workshop.
School booking
A customised session delivered for your full staff, on your campus or ours.

Schools booking a group session can tell us the specific problems your team is facing and we will build the session around those, rather than delivering something generic.

04 CAREER GUIDANCE & PLACEMENT

Career Guidance & Placement

Choosing a direction is hard when everyone around you has an opinion. Our career guidance sessions help students and parents make that decision with actual information - aptitude, interests, realistic paths, and what each option demands.

Talk to a Career Counsellor

CAREER GUIDANCE INCLUDES

  • One-to-one career counselling for students and parents
  • Stream and course selection guidance after Class 10 & Class 12
  • Aptitude and interest exploration
  • Realistic mapping of qualifications, timelines and costs
  • Guidance for adults considering a career change into teaching

PLACEMENT ASSISTANCE FOR OUR STUDENTS

Students who complete our NTT programme receive placement assistance: interview preparation, resume and demo-class coaching, and referrals to pre-schools and playschools that hire from our batches. Our team also prepares you for what a demo class actually looks like, since that is where most first-time candidates struggle.

We provide active support and introductions - we do not sell guaranteed jobs, and you should be cautious of any institute that does.
