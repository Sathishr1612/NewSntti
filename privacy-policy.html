Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 20, 2026

Sowmya Nursery Teacher Training Institute (SNTTI) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information shared with us through our website, enquiry forms, course registrations, counselling enquiries, workshops, and other communication channels.

This Privacy Policy explains how SNTTI collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you visit or interact with our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with SNTTI, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • City and location details
  • Educational or professional information shared during admission or course enquiries
  • Information submitted through contact, enquiry, registration, or subscription forms
  • Payment-related information required for course registration or other services
  • Technical information such as device, browser, IP address, and website usage data

We collect only information that is reasonably required to respond to your enquiry, provide our educational and related services, improve our website, or communicate with you.

2. How We Use Your Information

SNTTI may use your information to:

  • Respond to course, admission, workshop, counselling, or career guidance enquiries
  • Process registrations and communicate course-related information
  • Provide information about classes, schedules, workshops, events, and educational programmes
  • Send relevant updates, subject to applicable consent and communication preferences
  • Improve our website, services, learning experience, and customer support
  • Maintain internal records and comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements

3. Communication and Marketing

By submitting your details through the SNTTI website, you may be contacted through phone calls, email, SMS, WhatsApp, or other appropriate communication channels regarding your enquiry or relevant SNTTI services.

You may request to stop receiving promotional communications at any time by contacting us.

4. Sharing of Information

SNTTI does not sell or rent personal information.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary with authorised service providers, technology partners, payment service providers, or other parties supporting our operations, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security measures.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or government authorities.

5. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, understand visitor behaviour, analyse website performance, and enhance user experience.

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that some website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

6. Data Security

SNTTI takes reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. While we take reasonable precautions, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, platforms, social media pages. SNTTI is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of those websites.

8. Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

SNTTI may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website, or applicable requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with the revised effective date.
