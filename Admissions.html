Admissions Overview

A simple, transparent path to enrollment.

Admissions are open for 11th Batch on 20th August 2026. The process is deliberately simple: enquire, talk to us, submit your documents, pay the fee, and start. No entrance exam, no agents, no hidden charges.

Below you will find everything you need – the step-by-step process, who is eligible, what it costs, what to bring, and answers to the questions we are asked most often.

Admission process

1

Send an enquiry

Fill in the enquiry form, call us, or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us which programme you are interested in.

2

Counselling

We'll discuss your background and goals. You're welcome to visit the campus and see a class in session.

3

Submit documents

Complete the admission form and hand over the required documents, either in person or via email.

4

Confirm your seat

Pay the admission fee to secure your place. You will receive an official receipt and your batch details.

Eligibility

Nursery Teacher Training (NTT)

Class 10th + Any Degree passed in any stream. Minimum age 16th + to any age limits. Candidates awaiting results may apply provisionally.

Teacher Training Workshop

Open to working teachers, teaching aspirants and school staff. No formal qualification required.

Individual & Marital Counselling

Open to all - no eligibility criteria. By appointment.

Career Guidance

Open to students from Class 10 upwards, parents, and working adults considering a change.

No Entrance Exam Required

No entrance examination is required for any of our programmes. Candidates who have completed schooling through open or distance boards are eligible for the NTT course.

Documents Required

Bring Class 10th to any Graduation marksheet and certificate, along with 2 passport size photocopies of each.

Originals are needed for verification only and will be returned to you the same day.
